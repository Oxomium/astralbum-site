Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

The game is coming soon: it will run in your browser, with nothing to install, and on Android. You will need to be at least 16 and sign in with a Google account, which keeps your progress. The game will be free and have no ads; optional purchases will exist, none will be needed to play.

Is it better to play in a browser or on Android?

The game is the same, and your progress is saved on the server: with the same account, you can switch from one to the other whenever you like. If you run into a bug, try another browser or the Android app. For now, optional purchases can only be made in the Android app.

Are the objects real?

Yes. The 30,000 objects in the game really exist: they come from public scientific catalogues, listed in the game's credits.

How do I delete my account?

In the game: Settings, then “Delete my account”; the account is erased right away. It can also be done by email, at support@astralbum.com, from the address of your Google account.
