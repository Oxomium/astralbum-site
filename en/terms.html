3. The game

Astralbum is a free game in which you collect cards representing real celestial objects, with optional purchases. The scientific data displayed comes from public catalogs (see the Credits). It is presented for educational and informational purposes; values marked “≈” are estimates.

The game requires an internet connection. It is played in the Android app, on an Android device compatible with the installed version, or in its Web version, at jouer.astralbum.com, with a recent browser. It is the same game: the same Google account finds the same profile, the same collection, the same friends and the same purchases, and all players play together (leaderboards, trades).

The Web version has no notifications, and purchases are not yet possible there (section 8). While it is being finalized, it may be restricted to certain accounts.

The game is available in French and in English; the language is chosen in the game.

6. Reports, moderation and sanctions

  • Automatic filter: when you choose your username, the game automatically checks that it does not contain a word that is offensive, hateful or reserved for the team (for example “admin” or “moderator”), including when written with digits or symbols in place of certain letters. A rejected username does not lead to any sanction: you simply choose another one.
  • You can report an offensive username by tapping the player (in the leaderboards, the suggestions and the friend requests) or from a friend's sheet, or report any unlawful content to us at support@astralbum.com, explaining what the problem is.
  • Each report is reviewed by a person on our team. An offensive username is replaced with a neutral username (for example “Astronome-0042”) and the player is informed of this in the game; they cannot choose another username. If your report led to a sanction, you are informed of this in the game.
  • Suspicious observations: using an automated tool to carry out observations is prohibited. The game automatically detects manual observations that are abnormally fast and successful and may then block observations for a few minutes (10 at most); the duration increases if this happens again on the same day. If this continues, our team is informed and may review the account.
  • In the event of a breach of these terms, your account may be warned, suspended or deleted, depending on the seriousness. Unless the law requires otherwise, you are informed of the decision and its reasons in the game or by email.
  • If you think a decision is a mistake, write to us at support@astralbum.com: we will review it.

The cards, dark matter, science points, boosters and other elements of the game are virtual content. You receive a personal right to use them in the game. They have no monetary value, cannot be converted into money and cannot be resold.

A celestial object belongs to no one: owning a card gives no rights over the real object, and no player can name a celestial object. The phrases “Object discovered”, “Object already discovered by …” and “first observer” refer to a first in Astralbum, not a scientific discovery.

11. Refunds, warnings and account freeze

  • Apart from the legal guarantee (section 10), refund requests are made to Google Play, which decides.
  • If a purchase is refunded when it has not yet been used, what it provided is removed from your account and a message tells you which items were removed.
  • Subscription: for 14 days, the right of withdrawal (section 9) allows you to stop the subscription and be refunded pro rata. A full refund obtained from Google Play when the subscription has been used, instead of this right, is treated as a refund of a purchase already used and gives rise to a warning.
  • If a purchase is refunded when it has already been used (boosters or stellar boosters opened, Clear Night activated), what remains is removed and what was used becomes a debt: your next boosters of the same type (including free boosters for standard boosters) first go toward repaying it. You also receive a warning for a bad-faith refund. The same applies to a refunded subscription that you have benefited from.
  • Payment disputed with your bank: Google Play may then ask us whether the purchase has been used, and we answer it (see the Privacy Policy); Google Play and the bank decide. If the payment is returned to you, the purchase is treated as a refunded purchase, under the rules above. None of this limits your rights with your bank, in particular for a payment you did not authorise: in that case, also write to us at support@astralbum.com.
  • At the 3rd warning, your account is frozen: you can no longer play; only the shop (for the purchases that settle your account), the information pages and account deletion remain accessible. To unfreeze it, buy back what was refunded to you although you had consumed it: your account is unfrozen 72 hours after you settle. After that, each new warning freezes it again.
  • At the 3rd freeze, your account is blocked permanently, with no possibility of settling it.
  • An account that is blocked, or frozen and not settled, is deleted after 150 days. For a frozen account, an email sent to the address of your Google account warns you one week beforehand: you can still settle until then.
  • If you think this is a mistake, write to us at support@astralbum.com.
