Who is responsible for your data?

[Name of the publisher], [registered address], who can be reached at support@astralbum.com, is the controller of your data within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What data is used, and why?

  • Your Google account (identifier and email address; Google also passes the name and photo of your profile to our login service, which keeps them with your account but which the game never uses or displays), to log you in, to send you the confirmation of your purchases and, at your request, the copy of your data, and if necessary to contact you about your account (moderation, refunds, deletion of an inactive or frozen account, important information such as a change to the terms or a data breach). Astralbum never sees or stores your password.
  • Your username, chosen by you. It is the only name visible to other players: do not use your real name.
  • Your game data: collection, game resources, missions, observations, research, appearance, trophies, friends, declined friend requests, blocked players, wishlists, trades and leaderboards.
  • The game language you have chosen (French or English), so that we write to you in that language (emails about your account).
  • The username reports you send and those concerning you, for moderation.
  • Your purchases: the Google Play transaction token, the order ID, the product, the price displayed, the country and date of the purchase, and what has been used from it, to verify and credit each purchase only once, to process your withdrawal from or cancellation of a subscription, and to process refunds (warnings, account freeze, see the Terms and Conditions). If a payment is disputed with a bank, Google Play may ask us about it: we then tell it whether the purchase has already been used, to what extent and when, together with the technical identifier of your game account that Google Play already receives with each purchase, so that it can answer the bank. We send it neither an IP address nor a location.
  • Technical logs (dates of actions, duration of each observation step, IP address at the hosting providers of the server and of the Web version), for security and to combat cheating.

This processing is based on: the performance of the contract (account, username, language, game data, purchases, emails about your account); our legitimate interest (moderation and reports, security, technical logs, combating cheating and fraud, responding to payment disputes); our legal obligations (purchase records, responses to the authorities). Without this data, the game cannot work.

Notifications (reserve full, research finished…) exist only in the Android app. They are scheduled by your phone itself: no data is sent for this. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

What the game keeps on your device: your session (so that you stay logged in), a copy of your collection and of the game settings sent by the server (to start faster), and a few choices (game language, notifications, tutorial step). The Android app keeps them in its private storage; the Web version (jouer.astralbum.com) keeps them in your browser's local storage, and your browser also caches the game files and the images already displayed. This information is strictly necessary for the game to work. Logging out erases your session and these copies; clearing the site's data in your browser (or the app's data in Android's settings) erases everything. The Web version sets no cookies and no advertising or audience-measurement trackers: this is why no consent banner is shown to you. All its files (program, fonts, images) come from our address and from the game server: none is loaded from a third-party site.

The age requested when the profile is created is used only to check that you are at least 16: it is not recorded.

Astralbum displays no advertising, uses no audience-measurement or profiling tools and sells no data. Some decisions are made automatically by the server, without human intervention: rejecting a username that contains a prohibited word, refusing to create your profile if you state that you are under 16, blocking observations for a few minutes after observations deemed suspicious (see the Terms and Conditions), applying the refund rules (warnings, account freeze) described in the Terms and Conditions, and the answer given to Google Play when a payment is disputed with a bank (we ask for the payment to be upheld if the purchase has been used, and we accept its refund if it has not; the decision then rests with Google Play and the bank). You can write to us to have a person review your situation.

Who can see your data?

  • Other players see your username, your avatar and your telescope level: in the player search (for a friend request), in friend suggestions (drawn at random, with priority given to players who have visited in the last 7 days), among their friends and in trades. In the leaderboards, they also see your rank, your league and your points. When you are the first to observe an object, your username is shown to the players who observe it afterwards (“Object already discovered by …”). You can block a player: they can then no longer find you or send you a request.
  • Your friends see your wishlist, and the objects in your collection that match their wishes or their trade offers.
  • Our service providers, who act on our behalf and only on our instructions: Supabase (hosting of the server, the database and the accounts), Scaleway (sending, from France, of emails about your account: purchase confirmations, the copy of your data at your request, before the deletion of an inactive or frozen account, or for important information), Google (Google Workspace, receiving messages sent to support@astralbum.com; Google Cloud, verification of logins and purchases), Cloudflare (hosting of the Web version's files, jouer.astralbum.com: to send them to you, Cloudflare receives your IP address and your browser's technical information, but no data from your account or your game) and GitHub (the team's backup tool for viewing or correcting an account when the administration page is unavailable; its log may contain your email address and your record, for 90 days at most).
  • Google, for logging in with your Google account and for Google Play payments (including, if a payment is disputed with a bank, how the purchase concerned has been used). Google also processes this data under its own privacy rules. In the Web version, you log in on Google's login page, to which your browser is directed, then you return to the game: no Google program is loaded in the game page.
  • The authorities, only if the law requires us to.

These transfers are governed by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, provided for in Supabase's data processing agreement, in Google Workspace's and in Cloudflare's; for the United States, Google and Cloudflare also adhere to the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework [to be checked for Cloudflare at the time of publication]. These safeguards can be consulted at supabase.com/legal/dpa, workspace.google.com/terms/dpa_terms.html and cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa.

For how long?

  • For as long as your account exists. You can delete it at any time in the game, on Android as well as in the Web version (Settings, then Delete my account): your profile, your collection and your history are then erased immediately.
  • An account with no login at all for 2 years is deleted. You are notified 3 months beforehand by an email sent to the address of your Google account: you only need to log in to keep your account. A current subscription prevents this deletion.
  • An account that is blocked, or frozen and not settled, is deleted after 150 days (for a frozen account, you are notified by email one week beforehand). A login with no profile created is erased after 7 days.
  • Records of purchases and of payment disputes (see above) are kept for 15 months, for all accounts, even after the account is deleted (they are then no longer linked to your profile), to handle refunds and payment disputes.
  • The log of emails sent (type of email, date, result; not their content) is kept for 15 months.
  • When you decline a friend request, this refusal is kept for 30 days: during that time, neither you nor that player is suggested to the other as a friend. It is erased sooner if you become friends.
  • Reports are kept for 6 months, the time needed to process them and to spot repeat offenses.
  • Observations deemed suspicious (date, duration, score) are kept for 12 months, to spot repeat offenses.
  • The team's actions on an account (viewing, copy of the data, corrected username, suspension, deletion) are recorded in a log kept for 12 months, so that we can account for them.
  • The detail of your observations (answers and duration of each step) is erased 7 days after the observation, and the observation itself (object, score, date) 45 days after; only your counters (number of observations, scores) remain.
  • The game's technical logs (cards received, boosters opened, currency movements) are kept for 12 months at most; the record of login days, for 60 days.

Your rights

You may at any time, by writing to support@astralbum.com from the address of your Google account:

  • obtain a copy of your data (right of access);
  • have it corrected (right to rectification) or erased (right to erasure);
  • ask us to restrict its use, for example while a dispute is being checked (right to restriction of processing);
  • receive your data (account and game data) in a machine-readable format, to pass it on to another service (right to data portability);
  • object to its use for the security of the game and for combating fraud (right to object).

We reply within one month; for a complex request, this period may be extended by two months, and we will then let you know within the first month.

The copy of your data (access, portability) is sent to you by email, to the address of your Google account, in a JSON file.

If you die, your account and your collection remain as they are until a relative asks us to delete them, or until they are deleted after 2 years without a login (see “For how long?”). You can also tell us in advance if you want your data to be passed on to a person of your choice.

You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr), or, if you live in another country, with the data protection authority of your country.

Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and only the server can modify your collection and your resources. No system is infallible: in the event of a data breach that concerns you, we will notify you as the law requires.

Minors

Astralbum is reserved for players aged 16 and over. It is up to you to state your real age: it is neither verified nor recorded. Parents can control or block purchases with Family Link or Google Play's purchase verification (no purchase is possible in the Web version).

Changes

If this policy changes materially, you will be informed in the game and by email at least 30 days beforehand.
