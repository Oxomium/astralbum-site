Delete my Astralbum account

This page explains how to request the deletion of your Astralbum account and of the data linked to it, even if the game is no longer installed. English translation provided for information; the French version prevails.

From the game (immediate)

  1. Open Astralbum and log in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm (SUPPRIMER if the game is in French). The account is erased right away.

This is also possible if your account is frozen, blocked or suspended: the button is on the screen shown when you log in.

Without the game (by email)

  1. Write to support@astralbum.com from the address of the Google account you used to play, with the subject “Deletion of my Astralbum account”.
  2. Give your username if you remember it.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days at most and confirm it to you by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and your username, your collection, your resources, your missions, your observations, your appearance, your trophies, your friends, your wishlists, your trades and your ranks in the leaderboards.
  • Your login (identifier and email address of your Google account at our hosting provider).

What is kept, and for how long

  • The records of your purchases (transaction token, product, price, country, date), detached from your profile: 15 months, to handle refunds and payment disputes.
  • The log of the team's actions on your account (including the deletion itself): 12 months.
  • The reports you sent: 6 months.

Subscription

If you have a current subscription, it is cancelled when the deletion is made from the game. For a deletion by email, remember to cancel it in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions), or ask for it in your message.

Deleting only some data

You can also request a copy of your data, its correction or the deletion of only part of it, at the same address. Your rights are detailed in the privacy policy.
